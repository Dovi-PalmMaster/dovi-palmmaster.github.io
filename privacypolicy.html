PalmMaster Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PalmMaster collects, uses, retains, and discloses personal information when you use our website. It covers the questionnaire and reading features, account sign-in, subscriptions, and customer support.

2. Information we collect

  • Birth details and questionnaire answers. When you complete the questionnaire, you may provide your date, time, and place of birth. You may also answer questions about your relationship status and future goals.
  • Palm photos and reading results. When you use the palm reading feature, you provide a photo of your palm. PalmMaster uses that photo to generate your reading. Your reading results may be associated with your account so you can view them later.
  • Email address and account information. We collect the email address you provide during the reading or purchase process. We use it to verify your sign-in and associate your purchase and reports with your account.
  • Support communications. When you contact us for help, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments you choose to send.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described in this policy for the following purposes.

  • Generate and personalize results. We use the birth details and answers you submit to personalize your experience and generate results. We use your palm photo to generate your palm reading.
  • Give you access to your account and reports. We use your email address to verify your sign-in and help you access reports associated with your account.
  • Manage subscriptions and paid access. We use transaction information to verify purchases, manage subscriptions, and determine which paid features are available to your account.
  • Provide support and resolve issues. We use the information you send us and relevant account or transaction records to respond to questions and investigate issues with sign-in, report access, or billing.

4. Transactions and payments

A third-party payment provider processes payments for subscriptions purchased on the PalmMaster website. The provider receives the information needed to process your payment.

When you start checkout, we record your selected plan, the initial payment amount, and the currency. When we verify a transaction, we use the order reference and payment status. The status may indicate that a payment succeeded, failed, was canceled, or is still pending.

We use these records to confirm whether your purchase was completed, activate paid access after verification, manage your subscription, and respond to billing inquiries.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files that a website can store in your browser. PalmMaster may use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes.

  • Website functionality. To support website features, such as keeping you signed in or remembering your preferences.
  • Website analytics. To understand how visitors use PalmMaster and identify areas for improvement.
  • Advertising measurement. To measure the performance of PalmMaster’s advertising campaigns.

You can use your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies needed for the website to function may affect features such as signing in.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We may share personal information with service providers that help us perform the following tasks.

  • Operate the PalmMaster website.
  • Process payments.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Analyze website usage.
  • Measure advertising performance.

The information each provider receives depends on the service it performs.

Legal disclosures. We may disclose personal information to competent authorities when required by law or in response to a legally valid request.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary for the following purposes.

  • Provide and maintain the PalmMaster service.
  • Manage payments and subscriptions.
  • Resolve disputes.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

After a deletion request, we may still need to retain certain transaction records to meet accounting requirements or handle payment disputes.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights.

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Restrict or object to certain uses of your personal information.
  • Withdraw your consent where we rely on it.

To exercise these rights, email palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act on your request.

9. Age restrictions

PalmMaster is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through PalmMaster, please email us at palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com.

10. Third-party links

The PalmMaster website may contain links to third-party websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices, and PalmMaster is not responsible for them. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website before providing personal information.

11. Changes to this policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version is available on the PalmMaster website. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
  • To the extent permitted by applicable law, your continued use of the website after we post an update means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to submit a privacy request, email us at palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com.
