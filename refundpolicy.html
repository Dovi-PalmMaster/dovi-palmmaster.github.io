PalmMaster Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how to cancel a PalmMaster subscription and request a refund. It forms part of our Terms of Use.

The website refund conditions below apply to purchases made directly through PalmMaster’s website. Purchases made through the Apple App Store, Google Play, or another third-party platform are subject to that platform’s applicable billing and refund procedures.

Nothing in this Policy limits any rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

2. Subscriptions and Automatic Renewal

PalmMaster may offer subscriptions with different initial periods, billing cycles, or promotional prices. The applicable price, subscription duration, renewal amount, and billing frequency will be disclosed before you complete your purchase.

Unless cancelled, your subscription will automatically renew under the terms presented at checkout. If an introductory or promotional period is offered, the subsequent renewal period and price may differ from the initial offer.

3. How to Cancel Your Subscription

Subscriptions purchased through our website

To cancel automatic renewal:

  • Sign in to PalmMaster using the email address associated with your purchase.
  • Go to “My Account” and open the “Membership” page.
  • Find your subscription and select the option to cancel automatic renewal.
  • Follow the on-screen instructions until cancellation is confirmed.

Please cancel at least 24 hours before your next scheduled renewal to avoid being charged for the next billing period.

After successful cancellation, your subscription status will be updated, and we will send a confirmation email to the email address associated with your purchase. Your membership will remain active until the end of the paid billing period and will not automatically renew.

Reports you have already obtained will remain available for viewing and sharing after your membership expires. Creating new readings or using other membership features will require an active subscription.

If cancellation fails or you cannot access your subscription, contact palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com promptly for assistance.

Subscriptions purchased through an app store

Subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play must be cancelled through the relevant store’s subscription settings. Please follow that platform’s cancellation requirements and deadlines.

Deleting the application, signing out, or stopping use of PalmMaster does not cancel your subscription. Cancelling a subscription does not automatically refund previous payments.

4. Seven-Day Refund Request Period

For an initial purchase made directly through our website, you may submit a refund request within 7 days of the purchase date.

Submitting a request within this period does not automatically guarantee a refund. Your request must meet the eligibility conditions below, unless applicable law requires otherwise.

5. Eligible Refund Requests

We may approve a refund in the following circumstances:

  • Unused paid services. You submit your request within 7 days of your initial purchase and have not viewed a paid reading report or used another paid feature.
  • Unresolved technical problems. A technical problem with PalmMaster prevents you from accessing or using the purchased service, and we are unable to resolve it after you contact us and provide the information reasonably necessary to investigate.
  • Duplicate charges. We verify that you were charged more than once for the same purchase. We will refund the duplicate amount.

If you suspect a duplicate or incorrect charge, please contact us promptly, even if the initial 7-day period has passed, so we can investigate.

6. Requests That Do Not Normally Qualify

Except where required by applicable law, refunds are not normally provided for:

  • Dissatisfaction with a reading, disagreement with its content, or unmet personal expectations after viewing a paid report or using a paid feature.
  • A change of mind after using paid services.
  • Failure to cancel before an automatic renewal.
  • Non-use of a renewed subscription.
  • Requests submitted more than 7 days after the initial purchase.
  • Ordinary subscription renewal payments.

These exclusions do not prevent us from investigating technical billing errors, duplicate charges, or circumstances in which applicable law requires a remedy.

7. How to Request a Refund

Send your request to palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com and include:

  • The email address used for your purchase.
  • The purchase date.
  • Your order number or payment receipt, if available.
  • A brief explanation of why you are requesting a refund.
  • For technical problems, a description of the issue and any relevant screenshots.

Please do not send your full payment card number, password, or verification codes.

We will review your request and notify you by email whether it has been approved. We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the purchase or investigate the issue.

Submitting a refund request does not itself cancel your subscription. If you want to stop future renewals, please also complete the cancellation process described above.

8. Refund Processing and Access

Approved refunds will generally be processed within 5–7 business days and returned to the original payment method.

The time it takes for the funds to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider. This processing period is separate from the time needed to review and approve your request.

If a purchase is refunded, access to the paid services or content covered by that refund may end. Continued access to historical reports following ordinary subscription cancellation does not necessarily apply to a refunded purchase.

9. App Store and Other Platform Purchases

For purchases made through the Apple App Store, submit refund requests through Apple’s refund process.

For purchases made through Google Play or another third-party platform, follow the refund instructions provided by that platform. If its process directs you to the developer, contact us at palmmaster.feedback@outlook.com.

The original purchase channel determines the applicable billing and refund process, even if you access PalmMaster on another device or platform.

10. Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this Policy from time to time. The latest version will be available on the PalmMaster website, with the updated date shown at the top.
  • Changes will not remove rights that applicable law gives you in relation to an existing purchase.
  • Where required by applicable law, we will provide notice of material changes.
